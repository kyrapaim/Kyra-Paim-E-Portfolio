The Journey

Trip Description and Overview

Where I went, what I worked on, and why it mattered.

Hello, I'm Kyra Paim! I'm pursuing a Computer Science degree with a minor in Brazilian Portuguese at the University of Florida. Over the span of six weeks, I studied abroad in Cape Town, South Africa through the UF International Center's (UFIC) partnership with EDU Africa. This ePortfolio documents the community I worked alongside, the application my team developed, and the personal and professional lessons I carried home.

Over the course of this internship, I collaborated with a local non-profit organization, the Princess Vlei Forum, to integrate an AI chatbot into their website. As a non-profit organization, they rely heavily on the local community and volunteers to keep the space vitalized. Building a chatbot for their website allowed for users to easily find out how they can get involved, or facts about the rich biodiversity at the Vlei. We had the privledge of stretching this goal further, and redeveloped their website to save costs and enagage users further. Beyond the technical work, the trip immersed me in South African culture, history, and everyday life. From the stunning view of Table Mountain every morning to the community and friendships I built overseas, this study abroad was one of the most enriching experiences of my life.

This portfolio is made with the intention to reflect on my international engagement and connect it to my academic and professional goals. It serves not only to capture my team's work, but how it changed the way I think about technology, collaboration, and the world.

What We Built

Chatbot and Refactored Website Walkthrough

A visual showcase of the chatbot my team designed and developed during the program.

The Princess Vlei Assistant

The Princess Vlei Assistant is a friendly River Otter, an animal native to the Vlei. The Assistant assists users navigating the website's contents. Along the bottom right corner of the website, the users have the option to speak with the chatbot. Opening the chatbot gives the user the ability to ask preset questions, or personally ask anything in relation to the Vlei.

  1. The Problem

    The old Princess Vlei Forum website was messy and confusing. Users would have a hard time navigating the site to find relevant information quickly. Princess Vlei needed a feature that would boost user engagement and help site visitors find information fast.

  2. Our Approach

    We needed to integrate an AI chatbot into a dated website that depended on a buggy website builder. Working for a non-profit, we had to make the chatbot free, reliable, and easy to manage for our non-technical clients. Our team applied for a Microsoft Azure Grant for Non-Profits, and succeeded in recieving $2000 of credits every year for free for the Princess Vlei Forum. This chatbot was integrated into the original website, and our team rebuilt and transferred the content to a new website. The goal of the refactorting was to take away the website builder dependancy, reduce costs for the organization, and build a cleaner, user-friendly platform to support community engagement.

  3. Key Features

    Our chatbot and new website is cost free! The system prompt limits the DeepSeek model to a certain number of credits a day, and once that credit is reached the Princess Vlei Assistant falls back on the free CloudFlare AI model. This ensures the credit for $2000 worth of tokens isn't exceeded, allowing for the chatbot to continue being cost free for the client but still avaliable at all times. Users can speak with the chatbot to find out more about the Vlei and how they can get involved. People who are curious about wildlife can be linked directly to INaturalist links of animals found at the Vlei and when these sighting were recorded. Refactoring the website and hosting on CloudFlare Pages took away extra subscription costs the client was paying towards editing and hosting their site. To accomodate their nontechnical admin, we built a custon admin page for the Forum's website author to easily upload new articles and information for the chatbot.

  4. Impact

    After 6 weeks, my team built a reliable chatbot assistant focused on answering questions soley about the Vlei. Refactorting the site off of a website builder made Princess Vlei's website engaging, organized, and modern. We built around nontechnical admin, and established a way for the client to upload articles in a format they are used to. Overall, the chatbot and new website serves to attract potential visitors and volunteers through an easier user experience.

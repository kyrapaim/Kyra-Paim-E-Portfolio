Trip & Project Overview

A snapshot of where I went, what I worked on, and why it mattered.

[YOUR NAME HERE] — a [YOUR MAJOR] student at the University of Florida — spent six weeks studying abroad in Cape Town, South Africa through the UF International Center (UFIC) in partnership with EDU Africa. This ePortfolio documents that experience: the community I worked alongside, the application my team developed, and the personal and professional lessons I carried home.

Over the course of the program, I collaborated with a local host organization to design and build a software application addressing a real community need. Beyond the technical work, the trip immersed me in South African culture, history, and everyday life — from the vibrant streets of the city to the breathtaking landscapes beneath Table Mountain.

The goal of this portfolio is to reflect on my international engagement and connect it to my academic and professional goals — capturing not just what I did, but how it changed the way I think about technology, collaboration, and the world.
